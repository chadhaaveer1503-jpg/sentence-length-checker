    How words and sentences are counted
    • Words are split on spaces, like Google Docs. Hyphenated words (well-known), contractions (don't) and numbers (1,250, 3.5) count as one word.
    • Words joined by an em dash or en dash (this—that) or a slash (and/or) count separately, which is where Google Docs differs from Word.
    • Lone symbols such as & or a spaced dash are not counted as words. Web addresses and emails count as one word.
    • A sentence ends at . ! or ? followed by a new sentence, or at a line break. Titles and abbreviations like Dr., e.g., U.S. and initials like J. K. don't end a sentence.
    • Sentences within 3 words of the limit are shaded amber so you can see which ones are close.
